Cyber Security Awareness

Why Online Safety Matters

Cyber security protects our devices and data from unauthorized access. Always stay alert against phishing and social engineering attacks.

Top Security Tips (Ordered List)

  1. Use strong, unique passwords.
  2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  3. Keep software updated.

Common Threats (Unordered List)

  • Phishing emails
  • Malware downloads
  • Weak Wi-Fi networks
